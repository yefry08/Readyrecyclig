Ready Recycling
01
The first automatic container

Ready Recycling will significantly reduce plastic pollution in Dominican communities by increasing recycling rates. Our smart bins will create new green jobs in collection and processing. Communities will benefit from cleaner environments, reduced health risks, and potential economic opportunities through recycling. The project will raise environmental awareness and foster sustainable habits. Additionally, data collected from our bins will help authorities develop more effective waste management policies tailored to local needs.

02
People benefits

Our initial deployment phase will directly benefit approximately 50,000 people across pilot communities in the Dominican Republic. As we scale, we project reaching over 500,000 people within three years through strategic placement of our smart recycling bins in high-traffic urban areas, educational institutions, and tourist zones. The indirect benefits will extend much further, potentially reaching millions of Dominican citizens through improved environmental conditions, reduced pollution in waterways, and enhanced public health outcomes. Additionally, our educational campaigns will raise awareness about proper waste management practices among a broader population segment.

03
How it works
  1. Pre-sort. Each module of the honeycomb takes one kind of waste, and its mouth only fits that kind: a round mouth for bottles, a slot for paper.
  2. Sense. A distance sensor in the lid measures the fill level, load cells weigh what is inside and a temperature sensor watches for fire.
  3. Compact. At 85 % the ESP32 drives a 12 V actuator that presses loose bottles down by about 65 %, so each bin holds more than twice as much.
  4. Alert. When even compacting leaves no room, the bin reports itself full over Wi-Fi, LoRaWAN or 4G, powered by its battery and solar lid.

Click the bin to drop bottles in, or press C to compact.

04
In the city
  1. Bins report. Ready stations on street corners send an alert to the collection centre the moment they are full.
  2. The app gathers. The collection centre sees every station's level in the Ready app, with alerts as they arrive.
  3. Smarter routes. The truck drives the shortest route through only the full stations, instead of visiting every bin on a fixed schedule.

Click any station to fill it and watch the truck plan its route.
